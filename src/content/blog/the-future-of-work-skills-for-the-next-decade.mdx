# The Future of Work: Skills for the Next Decade

Which skills are likely to matter most over the next decade and why adaptability alone is not enough without substance.

## Why this question matters

The future of work is shaped by automation, AI, global competition, changing business models, and
ongoing shifts in how teams collaborate.

## What skills are likely to endure

Communication, judgment, adaptability, digital fluency, problem solving, leadership, data literacy,
and systems thinking are likely to remain valuable because they combine human and business
relevance.

## How to prepare

Build a skill stack rather than relying on one strength. Combine domain knowledge with durable
professional skills and enough tool fluency to stay useful.

## Career relevance

Professionals who understand how their value is created and updated over time will be better
positioned than those relying on static credentials alone.
