# Cybersecurity Awareness for Everyone

A practical primer on cybersecurity awareness for non-specialists, with a focus on everyday habits that reduce avoidable risk.

## Why this matters

Most security failures do not begin with elite hacking. They begin with weak passwords, poor access
habits, phishing, careless sharing, and low awareness. Basic security behavior protects individuals
and organizations alike.

## What everyone should know

Professionals should understand phishing signals, password hygiene, multifactor authentication,
device security, secure sharing, and the importance of reporting suspicious behavior quickly.

## Simple habits that help

Use a password manager, turn on MFA, verify unusual requests, avoid sharing sensitive information
casually, and keep systems updated. Small habits provide outsized protection.

## Workplace value

Cybersecurity awareness is valuable in every function because nearly every role now interacts with
digital tools, sensitive information, or workflow systems.
