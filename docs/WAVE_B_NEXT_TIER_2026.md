# Wave B — Next-tier 2026 skills expansion

Research basis reviewed 2026-09-09: World Economic Forum Future of Jobs 2025 skills outlook, LinkedIn Skills on the Rise 2026, and Coursera Job Skills Report 2026.

## Selection principle

After the 20 P0 AI-era guides, broaden the library around the foundations and human/business capabilities that current market evidence says are rising alongside AI. Avoid vendor-only micro-skills unless they represent durable transferable capability.

## Priority B1 — technical foundations around AI

1. Cloud Services & Infrastructure — LinkedIn Canada IT ranks it #3; Coursera emphasizes cloud engineering as a core AI-transformation foundation.
2. Governance, Risk & Compliance Management — LinkedIn Canada IT ranks GRC #2; connects cybersecurity, privacy, AI governance and regulated deployment.
3. Data Quality — Coursera reports +108% YoY growth among Data learners and frames validation as increasingly important as AI automates technical tasks.
4. Data Cleansing — Coursera reports +103% YoY growth among Data learners; durable prerequisite for trustworthy analytics and AI.
5. Debugging & AI-Assisted Debugging — Coursera places debugging in the IT top ten and describes human validation as increasingly important.
6. Cloud Security — combines WEF's rising networks/cybersecurity demand with cloud infrastructure growth.
7. Identity & Access Management — foundational to cloud, zero-trust, RAG permissions and agent authorization.
8. Observability — increasingly important for distributed, cloud and AI systems; connects DevOps and LLMOps.
9. Infrastructure as Code — durable cloud/DevOps capability for reproducibility, governance and automation.
10. Data Engineering for AI — data pipelines, quality, lineage, retrieval-ready corpora and reliable AI inputs.

## Priority B2 — human and organizational differentiators

11. Cross-Functional Collaboration — LinkedIn 2026 highlights this among rising leadership/people-management skills.
12. Executive & Stakeholder Communication — LinkedIn highlights executive/stakeholder communication as increasingly important through uncertainty.
13. Mentorship — LinkedIn identifies mentorship among rising leadership and people-management capabilities.
14. Team Management in AI-Enabled Work — combines LinkedIn people-management growth with human-machine team design.
15. Critical Thinking & AI Validation — Coursera reports critical-thinking enrollment growth across Data, Software/Product, IT and GenAI cohorts.
16. Systems Thinking — WEF lists it among skills expected to remain important and grow toward 2030.
17. Resilience, Flexibility & Agility — WEF ranks this among the fastest-rising and most important differentiators of growing jobs.
18. Curiosity & Lifelong Learning — WEF places it among the fastest-rising skills.
19. Talent Management for Skills-Based Organizations — WEF places talent management in its top ten skills on the rise; LinkedIn reports employers increasingly prioritize skills over degrees and linear paths.
20. Technology Literacy — WEF ranks technological literacy third among fastest-growing skills.

## Priority B3 — business growth and product

21. Go-to-Market Strategy — LinkedIn 2026 highlights GTM strategy among rising business/revenue-growth skills.
22. Business Development — LinkedIn highlights business development as companies seek strategic growth and new channels.
23. Operational Efficiency — strong relevance to AI-enabled process redesign and modern IT/operations work.
24. Data Storytelling — useful bridge between analysis, stakeholder communication and decision-making.
25. AI Product Management — combines product discovery, AI evaluation, data/UX constraints, economics and responsible deployment.

## Guide completion standard

Every researched replacement should contain: substantive definition; why it matters; modern/economic relevance; contexts and industry variation where meaningful; realistic scenarios; skill-in-action; prerequisites/related skills; subskills; tools as categories rather than vendor advertising; evidence summary; beginner/intermediate/advanced actions; deliberate practice; progress measures; mistakes; development methods; employer evaluation; mastery signals; career/resume/interview relevance; and transparent last-updated metadata.

## Recommended execution

Build B1 first in two five-guide batches. Before creating a new slug, check the canonical library for an existing equivalent and upgrade that record rather than creating a duplicate. Then run the canonical merge and completion audit. B2 should follow because current evidence strongly supports human validation, collaboration, leadership and adaptability as complements to AI rather than alternatives to it.
